# Garmi Check

A humidity-aware heat-stress checker for outdoor workers in India. Built for Environmental Hacks (WeMakeDevs x AWS), Heat and Water track, 8 to 11 Oct 2026.

India declares heatwaves by the thermometer. Your body feels the wet-bulb. Garmi Check shows outdoor workers both, hour by hour, in Hindi and English.

Work in progress. See `todo.md` for the plan.
